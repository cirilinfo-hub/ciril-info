# CTABand

The violet band (observed): `color-bg-brand`, radius 40 (32 tablet, 24 mobile), padding `band-padding`, white heading ≥32px, 18px body at 92%, accent CTA right.

## Use
- Bands sit inside the container with rounded corners, never edge to edge.
- `shape` adds the faint diagonal tonal shape (`violet-400` at ~30%); flat is the default.
- White text on `violet-500` is 3.95:1 – keep band body copy at 18px+ and headings ≥24px.

## Props
`title`, children (body), `action` (default “Learn More”), `onAction`, `shape`.
